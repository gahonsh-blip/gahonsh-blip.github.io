Client Completion Links

1. Choose the client

Select the client whose details you want the customer to complete. Only clients in your organisation are listed.

2. Enter what you already know

Anything you enter here is saved on the client record and shown to the customer as read-only. The customer can only complete the fields you leave blank.
Employment and income fields are only requested when an application is attached.
When set, the customer must enter this code to open the link. Share it separately from the link.

3. Share the link

Send this link only to the client it was created for. Anyone with the link can complete this client's information until it expires or you revoke it.
-
Gahonsh Finance does not send this message for you: copy it, or use one of the share buttons, so you stay in control of delivery.

Send to all clients (one click)

Issues every active client their own secure completion link and sends the message to them, each with their own link, in one action. A client who has already been messaged, or whose file is already submitted, is skipped and listed — a repeat press never re-opens a finished file or churns a live link. Tick Re-send to everyone to deliberately issue and send a fresh link again. Clients without a number/email for the chosen channel are skipped and listed.
The client's name and their own link are always filled in from the server.
