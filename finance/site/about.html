About Gahonsh Finance

We are building a lending platform where the terms are clear before you sign, the money is handled carefully, and a question about your loan is answered by a person.

What we do

Gahonsh Finance provides secured and unsecured retail loans — personal, business, agriculture, education, vehicle and gold — with an online application and a transparent assessment process. You choose an amount and tenure, see an indicative monthly instalment before you commit, and track your application from start to finish.

How we lend

Lending is a promise in both directions: you promise to repay, and we promise to be fair about how we assess you, price the loan and recover it if things go wrong. That second half is the part customers rarely see, so we publish it.

  • Transparent pricing. Rates, charges and penalties are shown before you accept an offer.
  • No hidden charges. No application fee to enquire, and no penalty for asking a question.
  • Fair assessment. We assess on ability to repay, not on gender, caste, religion or background.
  • Fair recovery. If a payment is missed, we contact you respectfully and within the rules — see Recovery & Collections.
  • Data care. Your information is used to assess and service your loan, and for nothing else without your consent.

Our commitments

Standards we hold ourselves to

  • Every charge is disclosed in writing before you accept a loan.
  • Grievances are acknowledged within 48 hours and resolved within 30 days.
  • Recovery contact happens only between 8:00 AM and 7:00 PM, and only about your own loan.
  • We never ask for your PIN, password or one-time passcode.
  • You can request your loan agreement, statement and repayment record at any time.

Where we are today

This is a development-stage platform. The customer site, the application flow, the portals and the recovery conduct rules are all built and tested, but the platform is not yet a licensed bank or public lending service, and no real credit decision, disbursement or repayment takes place in this environment.

We would rather state that plainly than imply a licence we do not hold. When registration and compliance gates are passed, this page will say so and link to the registration details.

Regulatory status

Gahonsh Finance is not an RBI-licensed bank, licensed NBFC or authorised lender at this stage. Nothing on this site is an offer of credit, a sanction letter or a regulated financial service. All figures shown are illustrative.
