Cookie policy

What cookies this site sets, why, and how you stay in control of the optional ones.

What cookies are

A cookie is a small file a site stores in your browser. It can keep you signed in, remember a preference, or help measure how a site is used. Some are essential to make the site work; others are optional.

Cookies we use

Categories of cookie on this site
CategoryWhat it doesTypical lifeStatus
EssentialKeeps you signed in and protects forms against cross-site request forgery.SessionRequired
PreferenceRemembers your chosen language and region so you do not reset them each visit.12 monthsOptional
AnalyticsHelps us understand which pages are used, in aggregate and without identifying you.12 monthsOptional

Your choices

Essential cookies cannot be switched off, because the site cannot function without them. For the optional categories, you can change your browser settings to block or delete cookies at any time. Blocking optional cookies does not stop you applying for a loan.

Third-party cookies

Where a page embeds a third-party service, that provider may set its own cookies. Those are governed by the provider's policy, not ours, and we keep such embeds to a minimum.

More about your data

For what we collect, why, and how to exercise your rights, see the Privacy Notice.
