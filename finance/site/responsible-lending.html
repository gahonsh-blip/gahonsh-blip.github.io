Responsible lending

We would rather offer a loan you can comfortably repay than one that strains your budget. Here is how we put that principle into practice.

Borrowing you can afford

A loan should help, not trap. We assess whether a repayment fits your income and existing commitments before we offer credit, and we say no when it does not — even if you would prefer a yes.

  • Affordability first. We look at income, regular outgoings and existing debt, not just a score.
  • Clear total cost. You see the rate, the processing charge and the total payable before you accept.
  • No pressure. You are never rushed into a decision, and declining costs you nothing.
  • Cooling-off. Where a product offers a cooling-off period, it is stated in your agreement.

How we price a loan

The rate reflects the product, the tenure and our assessment of risk. Two customers with the same product can receive different rates, and that is stated openly rather than hidden in a fee. Every charge that applies is listed on the Rates & Fees page.

If your circumstances change

Job loss, illness, a bereavement or a natural disaster can make a repayment hard. Tell us as soon as you can. We would rather agree a workable plan than let an arrears position build. Options can include a revised due date, a longer tenure or a temporary arrangement, depending on your product and situation.

Fair treatment, every time

We assess on ability to repay, never on gender, caste, religion, region or background. Our conduct in recovery follows the same principle — the rules are published on the Recovery & Collections page and summarised in the Fair Practices Code.

Borrow within your means

Before you apply, ask what a missed instalment would mean for your budget. If a repayment would strain essentials, consider a smaller amount, a longer tenure, or waiting. A declined application is better than an unaffordable loan.

Our lending principles

  • Affordability assessed Income and commitments, not just a score.
  • Total cost disclosed Rate and charges before you accept.
  • No discrimination Assessed on ability to repay alone.
  • Support if things change Tell us early and we will look at options.
