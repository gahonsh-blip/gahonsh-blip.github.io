How the estimate is worked out

The estimate uses the standard amortising-instalment formula, the same one applied to a reducing-balance loan. The instalment is fixed for the tenure, and each payment covers the interest due on the outstanding balance plus a repayment of principal.

  • EMI — the fixed monthly instalment.
  • Total interest — everything you pay above the amount borrowed.
  • Total payable — principal plus total interest.

This is an estimate, not an offer

Figures shown are illustrative. They exclude any processing fee and are not a sanction, a quote or a commitment to lend. Your sanction letter carries the binding numbers.
