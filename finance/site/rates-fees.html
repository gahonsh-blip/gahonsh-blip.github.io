Rates & Fees

What a loan costs, in one place — indicative interest rates, tenure ranges and the charges that can apply. Your final terms are confirmed in writing in your sanction letter before you accept.

Indicative figures

Every rate and charge below is illustrative development data. The actual rate depends on your profile, credit assessment and the documents provided, and is confirmed only in your sanction letter.

Illustrative repayment example

A Personal Loan of ₹1,00,000 over 24 months at an indicative 11.50% p.a. reducing balance would have a monthly instalment of roughly ₹4,684, and a total repayment of roughly ₹1,12,417 over the term.

This example is illustrative and excludes the processing fee and any taxes. Your actual instalment is confirmed in your sanction letter and repayment schedule.

How charges are disclosed

  • Your sanction letter lists every charge that applies to your loan, in writing, before you accept.
  • Your repayment schedule sets out each instalment and its due date.
  • Penalties, if any, are calculated on the overdue amount only — never on the whole loan.
  • You can request a full statement of your account, and of any charges applied, at any time.
  • Charges are never deducted without being disclosed in the sanction terms or agreement.

Questions about a charge?

If a charge on your account is unclear, ask us before paying it. Our support team can explain how it was calculated, and the grievance ladder applies if you disagree with the answer.
