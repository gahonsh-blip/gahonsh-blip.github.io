Security & fraud

How we protect your account, how to spot a loan scam, and what to do if someone pretends to be us.

How we keep your account safe

  • Encrypted in transit Traffic between your browser and us is encrypted, so your details are not readable on the network.
  • Access is limited Only staff who need your record to do their job can see it, and every access is logged.
  • You control your login You choose your password, and we will never ask you for it.
  • We watch for the unusual Suspicious sign-ins and payment changes are flagged for review.

Six warning signs of a loan scam

If any of these appears in a call, message or email, stop and verify with us first.

A payment to a personal account

We never ask you to transfer money to an individual, a wallet or an unfamiliar account. Repayments go only to the account named in your sanction letter.

A request for your OTP, PIN or password

No genuine call from us will ask for a one-time password, card PIN, CVV or net-banking password. Anyone who does is committing fraud.

Pressure to act now

Fraud relies on urgency. We do not threaten you, set a same-minute deadline, or claim your account will be closed unless you pay immediately.

A loan fee before disbursal

We do not ask for an up-front "processing", "insurance" or "file" fee paid to a personal account before a loan is disbursed. Fees are disclosed in the sanction letter.

An offer that is too easy

Be wary of guaranteed approval, no-document loans, or an offer to fix your credit score for a fee. Those are not how lending works.

A link that is not ours

Our pages live under the address you were given for this site. Check before you enter any details, and never share a login on a page you reached from a message.

If you think you have been targeted

  1. Stop. Do not send money, share a code, or install anything the caller asks for.
  2. Do not delete anything. Keep the message, the number and any reference you were given.
  3. Tell your bank immediately if you shared account details or made a payment.
  4. Report it to us so we can warn other customers and check whether your account was affected.

We will never rush you

A real call from Gahonsh Finance is never a reason to panic. If you are unsure whether a call is genuine, hang up and call us back on the number below.
