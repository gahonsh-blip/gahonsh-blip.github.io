Careers

We are an early-stage team building a lending platform where the terms are clear and the customer comes first.

We are a development-stage platform

Gahonsh Finance is an early-stage product, not a licensed lender. Roles are advertised so that candidates can register interest for when hiring opens. We do not run a live recruitment process through this page today.

How we work

  • Small, accountable teams You own a problem end to end rather than a slice of one.
  • Customer-first by default Every decision is tested against whether it is fair to the person borrowing.
  • Written before verbal If we agree to something, it is in writing, and it is true.
  • Sustainable pace We plan work so it can be done well rather than late.

Areas we expect to hire in

Credit & Risk

Assessment policy, affordability and responsible lending.

Engineering & Security

Platform, payments, and protecting customer data.

Customer Operations

Support, collections and grievance handling.

Compliance

Fair practices, privacy and regulatory readiness.
