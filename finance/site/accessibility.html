Accessibility statement

What we have built in for keyboard, screen-reader and small-screen users, what we are still improving, and how to report a barrier.

Last reviewed: 5 November 2026

We want this site to be usable by everyone, including people who use a screen reader, a keyboard, magnification or a small screen. This page says what we have done, what we know is still imperfect, and how to tell us about a barrier.

What we have built in

  • A skip-to-content link on every page so keyboard users can bypass the header.
  • Semantic landmarks — header, nav, main, footer — and one h1 per page.
  • A visible focus ring on every interactive element, never removed.
  • Form fields with a real label and inline error text tied to the field.
  • Text and background colours checked for contrast, with a target of WCAG 2.1 AA.
  • Content that reflows to a single column on a phone with no sideways scrolling.
  • Respect for the operating-system "reduce motion" preference.

Known limitations

  • Some older tables scroll horizontally on a narrow screen. The scroll container is keyboard reachable, but we are still improving it.
  • The team portraits are decorative illustrations and are hidden from assistive technology; the name and role beside each one carries the meaning.

Standards

We aim at the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 level AA. We test with a keyboard, a screen reader and automated checks before we ship a change.

Tell us about a barrier

If something on this site is hard to use, write to accessibility@gahonshfinance.example with the page and what happened. We treat accessibility reports as bugs and aim to reply within 5 working days.
