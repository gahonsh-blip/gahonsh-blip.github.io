How it works

From an indicative instalment to funds in your account, here is exactly what happens at each step — and what you need to have ready.

Four steps from enquiry to funds

Every step is shown before you reach it, and you can stop at any point without a fee.

1

Check your options

Use the calculator to see an indicative instalment for the amount and tenure you have in mind. Nothing is submitted and nothing is charged.

2

Apply online

Tell us who you are, what the loan is for and how much you need. Most applications take about ten minutes to complete.

3

Verification

We confirm your identity, income and repayment ability, and may ask for documents. You can track each stage in your account.

4

Decision and disbursal

You see the final rate and charges in writing before you accept. If you accept, funds are released to your nominated account.

What you will need

You can start with just a few details and add the rest as you go. Having these ready speeds up verification:

  • Identity. A government-issued photo ID.
  • Address. A current address proof matching your application.
  • Income. Recent bank statements, and salary slips or business records where relevant.
  • Bank account. An account in your own name for disbursal and repayment.

How long each step takes

After your loan is approved

You can see your schedule, make a repayment and download a statement from your account. If anything changes about your circumstances, tell us early — see Recovery & Collections for how we handle a missed payment and what support is available.

No fee to enquire

Checking an indicative instalment and submitting an enquiry cost nothing. Any charge that applies to a loan is shown in writing before you accept it, and is listed on the Rates & Fees page.
