Help & Contact

Questions about an application, an existing loan, a repayment or a charge? Here are the channels, the response times, and what to do if something has gone wrong.

Ways to reach us

  • Call support 1800 000 000 (toll-free), Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM – 7:00 PM IST.
  • Email support support@gahonshfinance.example — we reply within one working day.
  • Report fraud or impersonation If someone claims to be us and asks for an OTP, PIN or payment to a personal account, report it immediately to support@gahonshfinance.example.
  • Recovery & collections Repayment difficulty, restructuring or a concern about a recovery contact — see the Recovery & Collections page.

Grievance redressal

A grievance is any complaint about our service, a charge, a decision, or the conduct of a representative. It follows a fixed ladder, and you can move to a higher level if a lower one has not resolved your complaint.

  1. Level 1 — Customer support Raise it by phone or email. We acknowledge within 48 hours and aim to resolve within 30 days.
  2. Level 2 — Nodal grievance officer If level 1 has not resolved it, escalate in writing. Acknowledged within 48 hours, resolved within 30 days.
  3. Level 3 — Regulator or consumer forum Available once the platform is a regulated entity. Not yet live in this development build.

What to include in a complaint

  • Your name and the phone number or email registered on your account.
  • Your loan or application reference, if you have one.
  • What happened, and when — dates and times if you have them.
  • What outcome you are asking for.
