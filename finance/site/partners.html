What we rely on to verify, assess, pay out and protect a loan.

Banking rails Payouts and collections run over established bank networks.
Identity verification KYC checks use recognised verification services.
Credit assessment Affordability checks informed by credit-bureau data.
Payments Repayments accepted through standard payment channels.
Document storage Records held with encryption at rest and in transit.
Customer support Support handled in-house, six days a week.

How we choose a partner

A partner is chosen for a specific job — verifying an identity, moving money, storing a document — and is held to the same standards we set ourselves for security and data care. A partner never gains the right to use your information for its own marketing.

  • Purpose limited. A partner sees only what its task requires.
  • Contractually bound. Data-handling duties are written into every agreement.
  • Reviewed. We review a partner's security posture rather than assume it.
