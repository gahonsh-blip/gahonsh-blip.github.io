Grievance redressal

One ladder for every complaint: support first, then the Grievance Officer, then the Managing Director, each with a stated response time.

Last reviewed: 5 November 2026

A grievance is any complaint about our service, a charge, a decision, or the conduct of a representative. Every complaint follows the same ladder, and you may move to a higher level if a lower one has not resolved it within the time allowed.

Level 1 — Customer support

Start here. Most issues are settled at this level.

  • Email support@gahonshfinance.example or call 1800 000 000.
  • Give your name, the loan or application reference, and what you would like us to do.
  • We respond within 2 working days and aim to resolve within 7.

Level 2 — Grievance Officer

If Level 1 has not resolved your complaint within 7 working days, or you are not satisfied with the answer, escalate to the Grievance Officer.

  • Write to grievance@gahonshfinance.example with the reference from Level 1.
  • We acknowledge within 2 working days and give a written decision within 15.

Level 3 — Managing Director

If Level 2 has not resolved your complaint within 15 working days, or you remain dissatisfied, write to the Managing Director's office with the full trail.

  • Write to md.office@gahonshfinance.example.
  • We give a final written decision within 30 working days.

What to include

To help us resolve it faster, include your name and contact details, the loan or application reference, a short description of the issue, the outcome you want, and any earlier reference numbers.

Complaints about a recovery contact

If a representative has breached our conduct rules — calling outside working hours, contacting a third party, using pressure or abusive language — tell us at Level 1 and mark it as a conduct complaint. Conduct complaints are escalated immediately and are reviewed against our Fair Practices Code.

Your right to escalate further

This is a development-stage platform and is not a licensed bank or a regulated non-banking lender, so there is no sector ombudsman route at present. Once the platform is licensed, this page will name the external ombudsman and the process for reaching it.
