Eligibility & documents

What you need to qualify for a loan, what to keep ready before you apply, and how we assess affordability — in plain terms, before you spend time on an application.

Who can apply

These are the standard conditions. The final decision always depends on your own profile and documents.

1

Age

You are 21 or older when you apply, and within the age limit at the end of the tenure you choose.

2

Residency

You are a resident of India with a valid identity and address proof. See the note for NRI applicants below.

3

Income

You have a regular source of income — salary, business, professional or agricultural — that supports the instalment.

4

Bank account

You hold a bank account in your own name, for disbursal of the loan and for repayment.

Documents to keep ready

You can start an application with a few details and add documents as you go. Having these ready shortens verification. Only copies are needed at the start; originals are checked later where the product requires it.

Standard documents by category
CategoryWhat is accepted
IdentityA government-issued photo ID, such as a passport, driving licence, voter ID or a national identity card.
AddressA current proof matching your application, such as a utility bill, rental agreement or bank statement.
IncomeRecent bank statements, and salary slips or business records where relevant. Self-employed and business applicants may be asked for tax filings.
BankProof of a bank account in your own name, such as a cancelled cheque or a bank statement.
Product-specificSecured products may need extra papers — for example vehicle papers for a vehicle loan, or land records for an agriculture loan.

How eligibility is assessed

Eligibility is not only about a credit score. We look at whether the instalment fits your income after your existing obligations, so a loan does not become a burden. The assessment considers your income, your regular outgoings, the amount and tenure you have asked for, and your repayment record.

  • Affordability. The instalment should leave a reasonable margin for living costs.
  • Existing obligations. Current loans and credit commitments are taken into account.
  • Stability. A steady income and a settled address help.
  • Record. Your repayment history, where available, informs the decision.

Self-employed, farmers and students

The documents differ by how you earn. Self-employed and business applicants usually provide business proof and bank statements; farmers provide land records or a tenancy agreement and a cropping plan; students may apply for an education loan with a co-applicant and an admission letter. Read Responsible lending for how we approach affordability.

No fee to apply

Submitting an application and checking an indicative instalment cost nothing. Any charge that applies to a loan is shown in writing before you accept it, and is listed on the Rates & Fees page.
