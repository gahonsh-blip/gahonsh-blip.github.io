Help Centre

Answers to the questions we are asked most, grouped by applying, repayment, and account security.

Applying

Start from the application form on the home page. It asks for your personal details, the loan you want, and then lets you review everything before you submit. You can see an indicative instalment as you go.
Identity and address proof, recent bank statements, and proof of income or business. Secured products such as vehicle and gold loans also need the asset for valuation. We list the exact set before you start so you upload each document once.
Most complete applications receive an initial decision within 24 hours. Secured or complex cases can take longer, and we will tell you if yours will.
No. Applying is free. Any processing fee is disclosed in your sanction letter before you accept the offer, and is listed on the Rates & Fees page.

Rates and repayment

The rate depends on the product, the amount and tenure, and your credit assessment. The range for each product is on the Rates & Fees page, and the exact rate is confirmed in your sanction letter.
Yes. You can part-pay or close the loan ahead of schedule. Any foreclosure charge is listed in the sanction letter and on the Rates & Fees page, and is applied exactly as disclosed.
Contact us before the due date if you can. We will explain your options — a revised date, a restructured schedule, or a settlement where appropriate. See the Recovery & Collections page for what we may and may not do.

Account and security

Use the Customer Sign In page with the email or mobile number on your application. If you are new, create an account first.
Never. We will not ask for your one-time password, your card PIN or your net-banking password, and we will not ask you to move money to a personal account. If someone does, it is a fraud — see Security & Fraud.
Your information is used only to assess and service your loan. Our Privacy Notice explains what we hold and how to ask for a copy or a correction.

Still need help?

Our support desk is open Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM – 7:00 PM IST.
