Privacy Notice

This notice explains what personal information we collect when you use this site, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

Last updated: 4 November 2026. Development build — this notice describes the intended production handling and will be finalised with legal review before launch.

Information we collect

We collect only what we need to assess an application and service a loan:

  • Details you give us — name, email, mobile number, loan amount, tenure, purpose and employment status.
  • Identity and address proof — documents you upload during application or onboarding.
  • Financial information — income, bank statements and repayment history, where you provide them.
  • Technical information — a session identifier in your browser, and security logs of access to your account.

How we use it

  • To assess your application and, where applicable, make a credit decision.
  • To service the loan — statements, repayments, reminders and support.
  • To meet legal, regulatory and audit obligations.
  • To prevent fraud, and to keep your account and our platform secure.
  • To contact you about your account, and about a repayment if one is missed.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing without your consent.

Who we share it with

We share personal information only where it is necessary:

  • With service providers who help us operate the platform, under contract and only for that purpose.
  • With credit bureaux and identity-verification providers, where you authorise it and the law requires it.
  • With a recovery agent, only if a loan falls into arrears, only after written notice to you, and only the minimum needed.
  • With regulators, courts or law enforcement, where the law requires it.

How we protect it

  • Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (TLS) and stored encrypted at rest.
  • Access is limited to authorised staff who need it to do their job.
  • Passwords are stored only as salted hashes — never in plain text.
  • Account access is protected by authentication, with email OTP as a second factor.
  • We never ask for your PIN, password or one-time passcode, and we never ask you to pay into a personal account.

How long we keep it

We keep personal information for as long as your account is active and for the period afterwards that law and regulation require, then delete or anonymise it. Retention periods are reviewed as part of our compliance cycle.

Your choices and rights

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you, and request a copy.
  • Ask us to correct information that is wrong or out of date.
  • Withdraw consent for optional uses, such as non-essential messages.
  • Ask us to delete information we are not required to keep.
  • Ask for your loan agreement, statement and repayment record at any time.

To exercise any of these, contact us through Help & Contact. We will respond within the times set out in our grievance process.

Cookies and local storage

This site uses your browser's local storage for a session identifier and your language preference. It does not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

Changes to this notice

If we change how we handle personal information, we will update this page and the date at the top, and, where the change is significant, tell you directly.

Contact

For any question about this notice or your personal information, write to support@gahonshfinance.example, or use the channels on the Help & Contact page.

Our security promise

We never ask for your PIN, password or one-time passcode by phone, email or SMS. If someone does, do not share it — report it to us immediately.
