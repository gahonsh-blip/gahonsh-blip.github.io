Loan products

Six ways to borrow, each with the amount range, tenure, indicative rate and eligibility set out before you apply.

Six products, one transparent process

Every product shares the same journey: an indicative quote before you commit, a written sanction with the rate and charges, and a single place to track repayment.

Indicative figures

The amounts, tenures and rates below are illustrative development data. The actual offer depends on your profile and documents and is confirmed only in your sanction letter. See Rates & Fees for the full list.

Personal Loan

For weddings, travel, medical needs or any planned expense.

Amount
₹25,000 – ₹5,00,000
Tenure
6 – 48 months
Indicative rate
11.50% p.a.

Who can apply

  • Salaried or self-employed applicants aged 21 and above
  • A regular source of income and a valid bank account
  • Identity and address proof, plus recent bank statements

An unsecured loan, so no asset is pledged. Approval depends on your income, existing obligations and credit assessment.

Business Loan

Working capital to grow your shop, trading or services business.

Amount
₹50,000 – ₹15,00,000
Tenure
12 – 60 months
Indicative rate
13.25% p.a.

Who can apply

  • A registered business, shop or trading activity
  • At least one year of trading history
  • Business proof, bank statements and tax filings where available

Working-capital finance for stock, equipment or day-to-day operations. The amount offered reflects your business cash flow, not just your turnover.

Agriculture Loan

Seasonal finance for seeds, equipment and crop-cycle expenses.

Amount
₹20,000 – ₹8,00,000
Tenure
6 – 36 months
Indicative rate
9.75% p.a.

Who can apply

  • Land records or a tenancy agreement
  • A cropping or activity plan for the season
  • Identity and address proof

Designed around the crop cycle, with repayment options that can follow the harvest rather than a fixed calendar month.

Apply for this loan

Education Loan

Support for tuition, admission and study-related costs.

Amount
₹50,000 – ₹12,00,000
Tenure
12 – 72 months
Indicative rate
10.50% p.a.

Who can apply

  • Admission or an offer letter from a recognised institution
  • A co-applicant is normally required
  • Fee structure and identity proof

Covers tuition and study-related expenses. Repayment can begin after a moratorium, so the loan starts once the course is under way.

Apply for this loan

Vehicle Loan

Two-wheeler and commercial vehicle finance.

Amount
₹40,000 – ₹10,00,000
Tenure
12 – 60 months
Indicative rate
10.25% p.a.

Who can apply

  • The vehicle to be financed, new or used
  • Identity and address proof
  • Proof of income to support the instalment

A secured loan against the vehicle, so the rate is typically lower than an unsecured one. The vehicle remains the security until the loan is closed.

Apply for this loan

Gold Loan

Quick secured finance against eligible gold ornaments.

Amount
₹15,000 – ₹6,00,000
Tenure
3 – 24 months
Indicative rate
9.25% p.a.

Who can apply

  • Eligible gold ornaments for valuation
  • Identity and address proof
  • A valuation appointment before sanction

A secured loan, valued on the day of sanction. The ornaments are kept safely and returned once the loan is closed.
