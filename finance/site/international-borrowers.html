International & NRI borrowers

Gahonsh Finance is built for borrowers with an international footprint. Here is what that means in this build — honestly, including the limits.

Lending today

Lending is offered in India only. The language and region switcher at the top of every page lets you preview how the site reads in other regions and currencies, but a region other than India is a preview for this development build — not an offer of credit.

If you are an NRI

Non-resident Indians can hold an Indian bank account and a PAN, which are the starting point for most applications. Where a product allows it, an NRI applicant normally provides the same identity, address and income documents as a resident, plus proof of overseas status and, for secured products, a power of attorney for the co-applicant or representative in India.

  • Identity and residency. Passport, visa or residence permit, and PAN.
  • Address. Overseas address proof and an Indian correspondence address.
  • Income. Overseas income proof and, where relevant, an Indian co-applicant's income.
  • Banking. An NRE or NRO account for disbursal and repayment.

Regions and currencies shown

The region switch changes the currency label shown on the site so you can read the indicative figures in a familiar unit. It does not change the underlying product, which is priced in Indian rupees.

Working with a co-applicant abroad

When a co-applicant lives outside India, the paperwork is signed and attested in the usual way for cross-border documents. Our support desk can explain the sequence before you start, so nothing is signed in the wrong order. See Help & Contact for the channels, and Eligibility & documents for the standard checklist.

Always written down

Whatever your location, every rate and charge is disclosed in writing in your sanction letter before you accept. Nothing is agreed only on a call.
