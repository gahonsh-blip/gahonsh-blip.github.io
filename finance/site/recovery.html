Recovery & Collections

A missed repayment is a serious matter, and we treat it as one. It is also fixable, and it is never a reason for you to be treated without dignity. Our team contacts you only to find a lawful, workable way forward. Everything we do is documented, time-bound and governed by a written code of conduct that every representative is bound by.

Our commitment to you

Gahonsh Finance does not use intimidation, threats, public shaming or repeated unwanted calls to recover a loan. We follow the spirit of the Reserve Bank of India's Fair Practices Code and its recovery-agent conduct rules: recovery happens, but borrower dignity is not negotiable. If you are ever unsure whether a call or message is genuine, stop, and contact us on the number published on this page — we will confirm it for you.

How we contact you

We reach you only through the phone number and email address registered on your account, and only to discuss your own loan — never with your family, neighbours, employer or referees.

Contact rules we hold ourselves to
Rule What it means for you
Calling hours We call between 8:00 AM and 7:00 PM only. Outside those hours, no recovery contact.
Identification Every representative states their name, their role and that they are calling from Gahonsh Finance, and carries a written authorisation on request.
Who we speak to You, or a co-applicant or guarantor on your loan. Nobody else.
Language and tone Business language only — no abuse, no threats, no pressure tactics, no shouting.
Frequency No persistent or repeated calling. If you ask us to pause contact while you arrange funds, we record that request.
Record keeping Recovery calls and visits are logged. You may ask for a record of the contact made on your account.
Your data Your loan details are never shared with third parties, posted publicly, or used to embarrass you.

What we will never do

These actions are prohibited by our own policy and by Indian law. If any person claiming to represent us does any of them, tell us immediately — see how to escalate.

  • Threaten you, your family or your property, verbally or in writing.
  • Use abusive, insulting or coercive language.
  • Call before 8:00 AM or after 7:00 PM, or call persistently at odd hours.
  • Visit your home or workplace without prior intimation, or force entry.
  • Contact your relatives, friends, colleagues or neighbours about your loan.
  • Publish or share your name, photograph or loan details to shame you.
  • Make false or misleading statements about the consequences of non-payment.
  • Ask for your PIN, password or one-time passcode — for any reason.
  • Demand cash, or payment into any account other than the one named in your agreement.

Beware of impersonation

Fraudsters sometimes pose as recovery agents. We never ask for your PIN, OTP or password, and we never ask you to pay into a personal account. If you receive such a request, do not pay, and report it to us at support@gahonshfinance.example.

What happens if a repayment is missed

Our process is staged and governed by policy. At each stage you are informed in writing, and you can stop the process by paying or by agreeing a revised plan with us. You will always be told what stage your account has reached and what your options are before anything changes.

  1. Reminder A courtesy SMS and email on the due date, and again if the instalment is unpaid.
  2. Outreach call A representative calls within calling hours to understand the position and offer options.
  3. Restructuring offer If you are in genuine difficulty, we assess a revised tenure, a temporary reduced instalment, or a repayment holiday where policy allows.
  4. Written notice If the account stays overdue, you receive a written notice of default setting out the amount, the charges and your options.
  5. Formal recovery Only as a last resort, after every other option has been offered and declined, and only through lawful means. Any recovery agent engaged is trained, authorised and bound by the same rules on this page.

Your rights as a borrower

  • To be treated with dignity, and never to be harassed or intimidated.
  • To be contacted only between 8:00 AM and 7:00 PM, and only about your own account.
  • To know the exact amount outstanding, and how it was calculated, on request.
  • To ask for a record of the recovery contact made on your account.
  • To ask for a copy of your loan agreement, statement and sanction terms at any time.
  • To request restructuring or a settlement discussion if you cannot pay as agreed.
  • To refuse entry to your home, and to ask a visitor to leave and correspond in writing instead.
  • To escalate a grievance through our internal ladder, and then to the regulator.
  • To be told, in writing, before any recovery agent is appointed to your account.

How to escalate a concern

If a recovery interaction did not meet the standards on this page, escalate it. Each level has a fixed response time, and the levels are cumulative — you can go straight to a higher level if a lower one has not resolved your complaint.

Grievance escalation ladder
Level Who Response time
1 — Support Customer support team Acknowledged in 48 hours, resolved in 30 days
2 — Nodal officer Designated grievance officer Acknowledged in 48 hours, resolved in 30 days

This is a development build, so the platform is not yet a regulated lender and the regulator level is not yet live. The internal levels are wired and testable.

If you are struggling to pay

Tell us early. A missed instalment is far easier to resolve before it compounds, and contacting us is the fastest way to protect your credit record. We can discuss a revised tenure, a lower instalment for a period, or, in hardship cases, a settlement — subject to policy and to a documented assessment of your situation. Contact us before you miss a payment rather than after, and ask for a repayment plan in writing.

Act early — it is in your interest

A request for a revised plan is assessed on its merits and on the information you provide. The earlier you raise it, the more options remain open. Waiting until the account is heavily overdue reduces what we can lawfully offer, and costs you more in charges.

Meet the recovery & collections team

One accountable desk per stage of recovery, with a named officer responsible for your case. Every representative is trained on borrower rights and the Fair Practices Code, and is bound by the conduct rules on this page before they may contact a customer. Their remit is to resolve the account lawfully — nothing more.

  • Trained and authorised Every representative is trained on borrower rights and the Fair Practices Code, and carries a written authorisation.
  • Calls between 8:00 AM and 7:00 PM No contact outside those hours, and no persistent or repeated calling.
  • Logged and auditable Calls and visits are recorded, and you may request the record of contact on your account.
  • One named owner per case A single accountable officer handles your account, so you always know who to speak to.
