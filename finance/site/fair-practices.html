Fair Practices Code

The standards we hold ourselves to on pricing, communication, data and the treatment of customers in difficulty.

Last reviewed: 5 November 2026

Our commitment

This code sets out how we lend, how we communicate, and how we treat a customer who is struggling to repay. It applies to every employee, agent and partner who acts for Gahonsh Finance. Where this code and a legal requirement differ, the legal requirement wins.

Before you borrow

  • We describe the product in plain language and give you the amount range, tenure, indicative rate and every fee before you commit.
  • We show an indicative monthly instalment so you can judge affordability for yourself.
  • We do not pressurise you to borrow more than you asked for, and we do not charge for an application.
  • We tell you what documents we need and why, and we collect only what the assessment requires.

The sanction letter

Before any money moves, you receive a written sanction that states the amount, the annualised rate, the tenure, the instalment, the repayment schedule and every fee. Nothing is deducted that is not in that letter. If a term changes, we issue a revised letter and ask you to accept it.

How we contact you

  • We call only between 8:00 AM and 7:00 PM, and only about your account.
  • We identify ourselves, the company and the reason for the call.
  • We do not call your family, friends, neighbours or employer about a debt, and we never post about it publicly.
  • We do not use threats, abuse, intimidation or repeated calling to pressure a payment.

If you cannot pay

Tell us early. We will explain the options — a revised date, a restructured schedule, or a settlement where that is appropriate — and put any agreed change in writing. See the Recovery & Collections page for the full process and your rights.

Your data

We use your information to assess and service the loan, and for nothing else without your consent. We do not sell it. Our Privacy Notice explains what we hold, why, and how to ask for a copy or a correction.

Complaints

If we fall short of this code, tell us. The Grievance Redressal page sets out the three levels of escalation and the time we allow ourselves at each.

In short

No hidden charges. Calls only in working hours. No contact with third parties about a debt. A written record of anything we agree.
