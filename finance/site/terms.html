Terms of Use

These terms govern your use of the Gahonsh Finance website and the loan application you may submit through it.

This is a development environment

Gahonsh Finance is not an RBI-licensed bank, licensed NBFC or authorised lender at this stage. Nothing on this site is an offer of credit, a sanction letter or a regulated financial service. No real credit decision, disbursement or repayment takes place, and all figures are illustrative.

1. Who may use this site

You may use this site if you are at least 18 years old and legally able to enter into a contract. By submitting an application, you confirm that the information you give is true, accurate and yours to provide.

2. Your application is not a loan

Submitting an application does not create a loan, and it does not oblige us to lend. An application becomes a loan only when we issue a sanction letter, you accept its terms, and the loan is disbursed. We may decline an application, and where we do, we are not required to give reasons beyond what the law requires.

3. Information you provide

You are responsible for the accuracy of the information and documents you give us. Giving false or misleading information, or using another person's identity or documents, is a serious breach and may be a criminal offence.

4. Your account and credentials

  • Keep your password confidential, and do not share your one-time passcodes.
  • Tell us immediately if you believe your account has been accessed without your permission.
  • We will never ask for your PIN, password or one-time passcode by phone, email or SMS.

5. Fees and charges

Rates and charges are set out on our Rates & Fees page and, for a specific loan, in your sanction letter. Charges apply only as disclosed there and in your loan agreement.

6. Repayment and recovery

You agree to repay as set out in your loan agreement. If a repayment is missed, we contact you within the conduct rules described on our Recovery & Collections page. We do not use intimidation, harassment or public shaming to recover a loan, and any agent we engage is bound by the same rules.

11. Complaints
