Hire World-Class Verified Talent

Hire world-class verified talent directly, without agency mark-ups or long procurement cycles. Gahonsh lists independent specialists who have been reviewed for the skills they claim, so the profile you open is the person who does the work.

Every freelancer profile shows a headline role, a short bio, a skills cloud and an indicative hourly rate. Verified members carry a badge earned through a completed review of their submitted samples and references.

Freelancers can attach a PDF resume to their profile from the marketplace portal. Profiles that include one show a Resume button; signed-in clients can open the PDF in an in-page viewer or download it for offline review, while anonymous visitors are asked to sign in first so personal documents are not exposed to crawlers.

  • Web engineering — responsive front ends, application interfaces and integration work.
  • PDF and brand design — company profiles, catalogues, brochures and presentation decks.

How to hire through Gahonsh

  1. Search by name, skill or role to build a shortlist.
  2. Open a profile and send an invite from the contact form, or post a brief so talent can come to you.
  3. Agree scope, milestones and budget, then track delivery from your portal.

If you would rather publish a brief and let specialists apply, post a job and it appears in the Find Work feed within minutes. For agency-led delivery on larger scopes, review our services and pricing plans before you commit.

How verification works

A verification badge is not automatic. It is granted after a review of the specialist's submitted samples, references and claimed skills, which is why a verified profile carries more weight than a self-written description. Unverified members can still be contacted, but their work should be judged on the samples they provide.

Rates and engagement models

Profiles show an indicative hourly rate where one has been set, and "rate on request" where the specialist prefers to price by project. Small, well-defined jobs are usually best handled as a fixed price, while ongoing development, reporting or maintenance work suits an hourly arrangement. Either way, agree the scope and the milestones before work begins.

Getting the best result from a hire

  • Describe the outcome you need, not just the tasks, so the specialist can propose a better approach.
  • Share examples or existing files where you have them; context shortens delivery time.
  • Agree what "done" means, including how the deliverable will be tested.
  • Keep communication and file exchange in one place so nothing is lost.

If you would rather compare competing bids, post a project and let specialists apply. Read about Gahonsh to understand how engagements are managed from first contact to final handover.

Why clients choose to hire direct

Hiring direct removes the layers that slow a project down. You speak to the person doing the work, so scope questions are answered in minutes rather than relayed through an account manager. That directness also means clearer accountability: the specialist who agrees the brief is the specialist who delivers it.

The trade-off is that you take on more of the project management, which is why we publish guidance on writing a brief and comparing proposals. Agree the deliverable, the deadline and what counts as done, and a direct hire runs as smoothly as an agency engagement at a fraction of the overhead.

When you are ready to begin, contact us with the requirement or publish a brief so specialists can apply to you.
