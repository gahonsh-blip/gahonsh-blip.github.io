5. How the marketplace works

Gahonsh publishes projects and proposals and provides the tools to agree work. When a client hires a freelancer, the contract for that work is between the client and the freelancer. Gahonsh is not a party to it and does not act as an employer, agent or recruiter for either side.

Hiring on the platform creates a record of the agreed amount and milestones. That record is evidence of what was agreed; it is not a guarantee that either party will perform.

6. Fees, commission and payments

Creating an account, posting a project and submitting a proposal are free. Gahonsh does not currently charge a commission on marketplace engagements, and the client pays the freelancer directly for the work.

Fees for agency services (Excel automation, PDF and presentation design, website development and AI workflows) are quoted in writing before work starts, and are payable against the milestones in that quote. A quote is valid for the period stated on it.

If we introduce a marketplace fee in future, we will publish the rate on the pricing page and give existing users at least 30 days' notice before it applies to a new engagement. It will not apply to an engagement that was already agreed.

Accepted payment methods are bank transfer, UPI and international transfer, and every invoice is documented. Nothing is charged before a quote is confirmed in writing.

7. Revisions, refunds and cancellation

Every paid engagement includes the revision rounds stated in the quote. Corrections inside the agreed scope are free; a new scope is a new milestone and is quoted separately.

A deposit secures the schedule and becomes non-refundable once work has started, because it reserves delivery time. If a project is cancelled before work starts, the deposit is returned in full.

If a milestone is delivered and accepted, it is payable. If an agreed deliverable is not provided at all, the client may ask for a refund of the amount paid for that milestone. Refund requests are reviewed case by case, against the agreed scope and what was actually delivered.

Each paid delivery includes 7 days of free bug support. A defect in the delivered work is fixed free during that window. That support does not cover new requirements, third-party changes, or edits made by someone else after delivery.

8. Disputes between clients and freelancers

If a client and a freelancer disagree about scope, quality, delivery or payment, they should first try to resolve it between themselves in writing, within 7 days of the issue arising.

If that fails, either party may ask Gahonsh to review the case by writing to gahonsh@gmail.com with the project link, the agreed scope, the milestone in question and the supporting evidence (files, messages, screenshots). We will acknowledge within 2 business days and give a written view within 10 business days.

Because payments are made directly between the parties, Gahonsh cannot reverse a bank transfer or hold funds. Our role in a dispute is to review the platform record, state what the agreed scope shows, and recommend a resolution. A party who is not satisfied may pursue their own legal remedy.

Both parties agree to keep a dispute confidential while it is being reviewed.

9. Verification, fraud and suspension

A verified badge means we have reviewed the member's submitted samples, references and claimed skills. It is not a background check, a credit check or a guarantee of any specific outcome, and it can be revoked if the basis for it turns out to be false.

We may warn, restrict or suspend an account, and remove content, if we find or reasonably suspect false identity or impersonation, fake samples or reviews, bidding on your own project, payment fraud or chargeback abuse, harassment, spam, or attempts to move a transaction off-platform to avoid these terms.

Where it is safe and lawful to do so, we will tell the account holder what was removed and why, and how to appeal by writing to gahonsh@gmail.com.

10. Intellectual property

Ownership of the final deliverable transfers to the client once payment for the relevant milestone has cleared, unless the written agreement says otherwise. Until then the work remains the property of the freelancer or Gahonsh.

Each party keeps what it brought to the engagement: the client's data and brand assets remain the client's, and a freelancer's pre-existing tools, templates and libraries remain the freelancer's. The freelancer grants the client a licence to use any such pre-existing material to the extent it is embedded in the deliverable.

We may show a delivered project as a portfolio sample only with the client's permission, and never with confidential data.

11. Confidentiality

Both parties agree to keep business information, data and files shared during an engagement confidential, and to use them only to deliver or review the work. Client data is never reused for another project or shared with third parties. We can sign an NDA before sensitive material is shared.
