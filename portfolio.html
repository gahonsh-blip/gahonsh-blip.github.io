Inside the Gahonsh Freelancing Portfolio

The Gahonsh Freelancing portfolio collects real deliverables built for clients who needed spreadsheets, documents, presentations and lightweight web pages that simply work. Each entry below represents a finished, production-ready asset rather than a mock-up, so you can judge the depth of the work before you commission anything of your own.

Excel automation and finance dashboards

The largest part of the portfolio is Excel and spreadsheet engineering. Finance trackers, stock registers, attendance sheets, loan amortisation models and KPI dashboards are built with live formulas, native charts and macro-driven workflows. A typical workbook handles a thousand rows of live data, recalculates instantly and removes the copy-paste routines that slow a finance team down. Every sheet ships with named ranges, validation rules and a short usage note so your staff can maintain it without support.

PDF engineering and document design

Document work covers company profiles, product catalogues, invoices, reports and brochures. These are engineered for print and screen at once: consistent typography, correct margins, selectable text and a layout that survives export to different readers. Where a client needs recurring documents, the source files are structured so a single data change updates the whole booklet.

Presentation design and pitch decks

Presentation projects turn rough notes into clear, sequenced decks. Charts are rebuilt from the underlying numbers, visuals are aligned to a single grid, and speaker notes are included so anyone on the team can present the material confidently.

Web pages and marketplace work

Web assistance ranges from small landing pages to the freelance marketplace you are using right now. The marketplace lets clients post a job, freelancers list their skills and both sides track progress from a shared portal.
