Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

Welcome to GAHONSH FREELANCING. We highly respect your privacy and data security parameters. This policy outlines how we protect and process the information you share with us across our communication streams.

We only collect data metrics that are explicitly and voluntarily shared by you when requesting project quotes or consulting through our forms and interactive WhatsApp routing channels. This includes:

  • Your Full Name or Brand Identifier

  • Project Specifications, Requirements, and Data Layout Files

We implement secure data processing boundaries. Your proprietary business data, financial spreadsheet rows, client directories, or template structures are kept strictly confidential and are never traded, sold, or shared with third-party tracking networks.

Privacy Policy Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information Gahonsh Freelancing collects, how it is used and how it is protected. We collect only what is needed to answer an enquiry, deliver a project or operate your marketplace account.

Information you submit through our forms, chat widget or email is used to respond to your request, prepare a quote and deliver agreed work. Account holders on the marketplace have profile and project data stored so the platform can function.

We do not sell personal data. Access is limited to the people who need it to deliver a service, and data is retained only while it serves a legitimate business or legal purpose. You can ask us to review, correct or delete your information by writing to the address on our contact page.

See also our Terms & Conditions for the rules that govern the use of this website and our services.

We use cookies and similar browser storage only to remember functional preferences, such as your light or dark theme choice and your signed-in session. These are not used to build advertising profiles, and disabling them will not prevent you from reading any part of the site.

Some processing is carried out by third-party services that help the site function: a hosted database and authentication provider for marketplace accounts, a form-handling service for enquiry submissions, and messaging links for direct contact. These providers receive only the information required to perform their specific task.

We retain enquiry records while they are useful for delivering or supporting a service, and account data for as long as the account remains open. After that, records are removed or anonymised unless a legal or accounting obligation requires us to keep them for longer.

You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you, ask for corrections, or ask us to delete information that is no longer needed. Requests are handled through the details on our contact page.
