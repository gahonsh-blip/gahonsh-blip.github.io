Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains what Gahonsh Freelancing collects, why we collect it, who processes it, how long we keep it and how to have it removed. It covers this website, the marketplace, the chat assistant and our enquiry forms.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Gahonsh Freelancing, Jharkhand, India, is responsible for the personal data described on this page. Contact us at gahonsh@gmail.com, on WhatsApp at +91 88251 83628, or through the contact page.

2. What we collect

  • Enquiries: your name, email, the service and budget you select, and the project details you type into the contact form or send by email or WhatsApp.
  • Account data: name, email, role, headline, skills, hourly rate, bio, location and an optional resume file. Passwords are stored by our authentication provider as a one-way hash; we never see the password itself.
  • Marketplace activity: the projects you post, the proposals you send, the contracts you agree, the messages you write and the files you exchange through the portal.
  • Technical data: the IP address and browser information recorded by our hosting provider in standard access logs.
  • Local preferences: your light or dark theme choice, the chat assistant's history and, when the database is unreachable, a local copy of marketplace data. These stay in your own browser.

We do not ask for card numbers or bank credentials and we do not store them. Payments are made directly between the client and the freelancer through their own bank or UPI app.

3. Why we use it

  • To answer an enquiry and prepare a quote.
  • To create and operate your marketplace account, and to show your public profile to other members.
  • To run projects, proposals, contracts and the files exchanged on them.
  • To keep the marketplace safe: detecting fake accounts, fake samples, spam and payment fraud.
  • To send service messages about your account or a project you are part of.
  • To meet tax, accounting and other legal obligations.

4. Our basis for processing

We process enquiry and account data because you asked us to, so we can answer you and provide the service you signed up for. We process technical and activity data because we have a legitimate interest in keeping the platform working and safe. We process invoice records because the law requires it. You can withdraw consent for any optional use at any time, and we will stop unless we have another lawful reason to continue.

5. Who processes your data

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with advertising networks or data brokers. A small number of service providers process limited data on our behalf so the site can work:

  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc. / Microsoft) hosts the site and records standard access logs.
  • Supabase provides the database, authentication and private file storage used for marketplace accounts, proposals, contracts and resumes.
  • Formspree receives contact form submissions and forwards them to our email inbox.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare) and Google Fonts serve the icon font and web font. Your browser requests these files directly, so those services see your IP address.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) is used only if you choose to message us there; that conversation is also governed by WhatsApp's own privacy terms.

These providers act on our instructions and are not permitted to use your data for their own marketing. Some of them process data outside India, for example in the United States or the European Union. Where that happens, the transfer relies on the provider's standard contractual safeguards.

We may also disclose data where the law requires it, or where it is necessary to investigate fraud or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

6. Cookies and browser storage

We do not run advertising or behavioural analytics cookies. Browser storage is used for functional purposes only: remembering your light or dark theme, keeping you signed in through the authentication session token, storing the chat assistant's history, and keeping a local copy of marketplace data when the database cannot be reached. Clearing your browser storage removes all of it, and the site stays readable without it.

7. How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept for up to 24 months after the last contact, so a follow-up question can be answered. Account and marketplace records are kept while the account is open and for up to 12 months afterwards, so a closed account can be restored or a dispute resolved. Invoice and payment records are kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires. Server logs are kept for as long as the hosting provider retains them. Local browser data stays until you clear it.

8. Your choices and rights

You can ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, ask us to restrict how it is used, or object to a particular use. Write to gahonsh@gmail.com from the email address on your account and we will respond within 30 days.

Deleting your account. Ask us in writing and we will remove your profile, proposals and files, and close the login. Records we must keep for tax, accounting or an open dispute are retained only for that purpose. A file you sent to another member through a contract may remain in their copy, because it is no longer only in our control.

Your public profile. Public pages show your display name, headline, skills and verification badge. Your email address and phone number are never shown publicly, and other members contact you through the platform.

9. How we protect it

Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Accounts are protected by our authentication provider, and database access is restricted by row-level security, so a member can read only their own private records. Administrator access to account emails is limited to platform administrators. File uploads are scanned in your browser before they are sent; that scan blocks common unsafe file types but it is not a full antivirus guarantee.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the relevant authority as required by law.

10. Children

The marketplace is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, write to us and we will remove it.

11. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our processing changes, and the "Last Updated" date above will change with it. Material changes will be announced on the site before they take effect.

12. Contact and complaints

Email gahonsh@gmail.com, message +91 88251 83628 on WhatsApp, or use the contact page. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the data protection authority that applies to you.

Privacy Policy Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information Gahonsh Freelancing collects, how it is used, who processes it and how it is protected. We collect only what is needed to answer an enquiry, deliver a project or operate your marketplace account, and we do not sell personal data or share it with advertising networks.

What we collect and why

Information you submit through our forms, chat widget, email or WhatsApp is used to respond to your request, prepare a quote and deliver agreed work. Account holders on the marketplace have profile, project and contract data stored so the platform can function: a client needs to see proposals, and a freelancer needs to see the brief they are bidding on. Technical data such as your IP address is recorded by the hosting provider in ordinary server logs.

Who processes your data

We keep the number of service providers small and name them. GitHub Pages hosts the site, Supabase provides the database, authentication and private file storage, Formspree forwards contact form submissions to our inbox, and cdnjs and Google Fonts serve the icon and web fonts your browser downloads. WhatsApp is used only if you choose to message us there. These providers receive only the information required to perform their specific task, act on our instructions, and are not permitted to use it for their own marketing.

Payments

Gahonsh does not collect or store card numbers or bank credentials. Clients pay freelancers directly through their own bank or UPI app, and agency invoices are settled by bank transfer. Because payment details never reach our systems, they cannot leak from them. The marketplace records the amount and milestones agreed, which is a record of the deal rather than a payment credential.

Cookies and browser storage

We use browser storage only to remember functional preferences, such as your light or dark theme choice and your signed-in session. There are no advertising cookies and no behavioural analytics, so no profile of your browsing is built. Disabling browser storage will not prevent you from reading any part of the site.

Retention and deletion

We retain enquiry records while they are useful for delivering or supporting a service, account data for as long as the account remains open, and invoice records for as long as tax and accounting law requires. After that, records are removed or anonymised. You can ask us to review, correct or delete your information by writing to the address on our contact page, and we will respond within 30 days. Deleting an account removes your profile, proposals and files; records kept for a legal obligation are limited to that purpose.

Security and your rights

Traffic is encrypted with HTTPS, database access is restricted by row-level security so members read only their own private records, and public profiles never expose an email address or phone number. You may request a copy of your data, ask for corrections, ask for deletion, or object to a specific use. Requests are handled through the details on our contact page.

See also our Terms & Conditions for the rules that govern the use of this website and our services, including how disputes and refunds are handled.
