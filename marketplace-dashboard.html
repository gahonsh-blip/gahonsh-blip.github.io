Marketplace Portal and Dashboard

How the Gahonsh Portal Works

The Gahonsh marketplace portal is the single place to manage everything you do on the platform, whether you hire talent or deliver work. Sign in to see your own activity; the overview below explains what each area covers.

For clients

  • My projects — every brief you have posted, with status and proposal counts.
  • Proposals — compare bids, delivery times and approaches, then accept the one that fits.
  • Active contracts — work in progress, agreed amounts and delivery milestones.

For freelancers

  • My proposals — the bids you have submitted and their current status.
  • Contracts — accepted engagements and the amounts agreed with each client.
  • Profile — your headline, skills, hourly rate and verification badge.

Getting started

  1. Create an account and choose whether you are hiring or freelancing.
  2. Complete your profile so proposals and briefs carry the right context.
  3. Start from the Find Work feed or post a project.

Looking for people to hire? Browse verified talent. Want to understand how Gahonsh prices and delivers work? Read our services and pricing plans.

Account types and what each one sees

A client account lists the projects you have published, the proposals you have received and the contracts you have agreed. A freelancer account lists the proposals you have submitted, their current status and the engagements you have won. Switching role changes the view but keeps your history, so you can hire and work from a single login.

What the status labels mean

  • Open — a project is published and still accepting proposals.
  • Shortlisted — a proposal has been marked as a strong candidate by the client.
  • Accepted — the client has hired the freelancer and a contract now exists.
  • In progress — the contract is active and milestones are being delivered.
  • Completed — all agreed milestones have been delivered and accepted.

Keeping your profile strong

For freelancers, a complete profile matters more than a long one. A clear headline role, a short description of what you deliver, an accurate skills list and a sensible hourly rate will win more invitations than a wall of text. Clients, meanwhile, get better proposals when the brief states the deliverable, the tools and the deadline rather than a vague description of a problem.

Ready to begin? Start from the Find Work feed, post a project or browse verified talent.

Profile, verification and trust

Trust on the marketplace comes from two places: a verified badge on the freelancer side, and a written milestone agreement on the client side. A verified badge means a member's samples, references and claimed skills have been reviewed. A milestone agreement records the amount and the deliverables before work starts. Gahonsh does not hold funds: payments move directly between the client and the freelancer, so read the brief, agree the terms in writing, and check the client or freelancer record before you commit.

Keep your profile current as your work changes. A specialist who updates their headline, skills and rate as they take on new disciplines receives more relevant invitations than one whose profile has not moved in a year. Clients should likewise keep briefs specific, because a clear brief attracts proposals from people who can actually do the job.
